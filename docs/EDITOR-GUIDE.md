# Editing the Utah Athletic website

A cheat sheet for editing the site in the Studio: **https://utah-athletic.sanity.studio**

## How publishing works

1. **Your edits save themselves** as a draft while you type. You never lose work by closing the tab.
2. **Nothing changes on the website until you click Publish** (bottom right of the page you're editing).
3. **The website updates about 1 to 2 minutes after you publish.**
4. **To check it, hard refresh the page** so you don't see an old copy: Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows.

If the Publish button is grey, something on the page needs fixing first. Look for a red mark on a field, or on one of the tabs along the top.

## Where do I change...

The Studio opens on **Start here**: big buttons for the common jobs (new messages, registrations, adding a tryout, session or event, the home page, staff). **Structure** at the top shows everything, laid out like the website. **Website pages**: **Home page**; **Academy**, **Club**, **Recreation** and **Futures** (each opens to its **Page content** plus its tryout sessions or sessions; Futures also has **Registration page and waiver** and **Registrations**); **Adult: UPSL** and **Adult: Adult Recreation**; **Tryouts page**; **Tournaments & Events** (**Page content** and **Events**); **Network page**; **Contact page**. **People and places**: **Staff** and **Venues (fields)**. **Inbox**: **Form messages**, **Futures registrations** and **Sent emails**. **Settings**: **Site settings** (main email, header Tryouts button, social links, default share image, shared Development model) and **Region settings**. **Registrations** at the top shows players by age group and gender.

## Adding a tryout session

**The quick way: copy one that's almost the same**

1. Click **Academy** or **Club**, then **Tryout sessions**, and open a session like the one you need.
2. Click the **⋯** menu at the bottom right, then **Duplicate**.
3. Change what's different: usually **Date**, **Starts**, **Ends** or the age groups.
4. Click **Publish**.

**Starting from scratch**

1. Click **Academy** or **Club**, then **Tryout sessions**, then the **+** button.
2. Fill in **Program** (Academy or Club), **Region**, **Venue**, **Date**, **Starts**, **Ends**, **Youngest age group**, **Oldest age group** and **Players** (Boys, Girls or Coed). For a single age group, pick the same age in both age fields.
3. If the field isn't in the **Venue** list, add it under **Venues (fields)** first, then come back.
4. Click **Publish**.

## Arrows on buttons

Don't type an arrow (→) in a button or link label. The site adds it for you.

## Swapping a photo

1. Find the photo using the table above. For example: **Club**, **Page content**, **Photos** tab.
2. Drag the new photo onto the old one, or use the photo's menu to upload a new one.
3. **Set the focus point:** click the photo's crop button. Drag the small circle onto the most important part of the picture, usually a face. The site keeps that spot in view when it trims the photo to fit.
4. Fill in **Describe the photo** if the field is there, for example "U12 girls celebrating a goal". People who use screen readers hear this.
5. Click **Publish**.

## What the Studio stops you from doing

- **Em dashes (the long dash, often added automatically when you type two hyphens).** The site's writing style doesn't use them, so you'll see a red message. Use a period, comma or colon instead.
- **A tryout that ends before it starts,** or an oldest age group younger than the youngest. This catches typos before families see them.
- **Tournament dates in the wrong order:** an end before the start, or registration closing before it opens.
- **Placeholder links.** A yellow warning means a link still points to a stand-in address like ollie.example or example.com. You can still publish, but it's a reminder to put the real link in.
- **Deleting programs, regions, the Network page or Site settings.** The site's pages are built around them, so you can edit them but not remove them.

## Undoing a mistake

- **Not published yet?** Use the **⋯** menu, then **Discard changes**. The page goes back to what's live.
- **Already published?** Open the page's **history**: click the "Published" time near the top right of the page. Pick the version from before your change, click **Restore**, then **Publish**.

## If a change doesn't show up

Wait 5 minutes and hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R or Ctrl+Shift+R). If it's still not there, tell Jeff **which page you edited** in the Studio (for example "Tryout sessions, Club North U12 Boys, May 17") and roughly when you published.
