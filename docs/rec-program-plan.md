# Rec Program Plan (internal)

Working decisions for the Utah Athletic rec league. Internal only: this folder is not published to the site.
Last updated: 2026-10-06.

## Principles

- Rec is a soft intro and recruiting pipeline into UA Club and Academy.
- Keep players in small-sided formats with tons of touches until they hit skill benchmarks, then graduate them to higher levels.
- Fun first: big shots, lots of goals.
- Inflatable pitches are for jamborees only (not weekly league play) for now.

## Divisions and formats (U5 to U9)

| Division | Format | Keepers | Field (yds) | Goals (ft) | Ball | Roster | Referees |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U5 | 3v3 | No | 20 x 25 | 4 x 6 | 3 | 5 to 7 (target 6) | None, coaches guide |
| U6 | 3v3 | No | 20 x 25 | 4 x 6 | 3 | 5 to 7 (target 6) | None, coaches guide |
| U7 | 4v4 | No | 25 x 35 | 6 x 12 | 3 | 7 | None |
| U8 | 4v4 | No | 30 x 40 | 6 x 12 | 3 | 7 | None |
| U9 | 4v4 | No | 30 x 40 (up to 35 x 45) | 6 x 12 | 4 | 7 | None |

Game rules (all U5 to U9):

- Timed shifts on a horn (about every 4 minutes) for equal playing time.
- Bench players keep a ball at their feet.
- No-entry crease in front of each goal (prevents goal hanging).
- Goals count only from the attacking half (U7 to U9).
- Kick-ins, no offside, no heading.
- Optional later experiment: 2-point "rocket line" for long shots. Also considered: Funino four-goal format.

Uneven attendance (U5 to U6):

- 4 to 6 players: 3v3 with rotation.
- 3 players: 3v3, no subs, extra water break.
- Under 3: borrow from opponent's bench or a team on a bye. Never forfeit.
- Short team never plays down.

Decided against: two side-by-side 3v3 games per matchup (attendance makes it unworkable), keepers at U7 to U9, 7v7 at U9.

## League size and combining

- Divisions are created for every age; families register into their own age.
- Minimum per division: 4 teams. Target: 6. Keep team counts even (flex roster size to get there).
- If a division is short, combine **up only** (U5 into U6, U7 into U8, U8 into U9) using the older division's field and ball.
- Go/no-go deadline 2 to 3 weeks before the season. Coaches confirmed counts as part of go/no-go.
- Under the minimum overall: run a Saturday clinic plus scrimmage format instead of a league.
- Use the same age group calculation as UA club.

## Game day operations

- One paid field coordinator per field (checks teams in, starts games, runs the shift horn).
- One paid director of field coordinators on site each Saturday.
- Director and coordinators arrive 1 hour before the first game to set up goals and fields.
- Fields repainted every 2 to 3 weeks.
- Open question: coordinators were planned as 12+. Federal law generally sets 14 as the minimum for most paid jobs. Check with the Utah Labor Commission; possible structure is paid 14+, volunteer junior coordinators 12 to 13.

## Cost model (planning estimates, not quotes)

Assumptions: 8 Saturdays, 162 players (U5/U6 combined 6 teams of 6; U7, U8, U9 at 6 teams of 7), 6 fields, 3 hourly slots, 4.5 staff hours per Saturday, coordinators $13/hr, director $25/hr, plus 10% payroll.

Startup (one time): about $5,500 (goals, anchors, striper, balls, cones, first aid, staff shirts, tent, horn, 10% contingency).

Operating per season: about $6,450 (coordinators $3,100, director $1,000, painting $700, background checks $960, storage $400, supplies $300).

Per player: about $46 plus processing (uniform $22, state registration/insurance about $20 to verify, medal $4).

Break-even: about $89 per player operating only; about $105 with startup paid off over 2 seasons.

Not yet in the model: field rental, goal transport/storage (possible trailer $3,000 to 6,000), program director pay.
